# IRON GAMBIT — BRAND CORE
Upload this file to every agent Project. Replaces the previous version.

## THE NAME
Iron = the plates. Gambit = the calculated sacrifice.
Chess builds the plan, iron builds the body. Both reward people who think in moves, not moments.

A gambit gives up material to take position. The 5am alarm, the meal you prepped,
the night you left early — that's the trade. Not a loss. A price, paid early.

**Motto:** Give up the piece. Take the board.
**Headline:** Strategy is a muscle.

## WHO IT'S FOR
25–40. Trains seriously, has for years. Spends on quality because they've learned
what cheap costs. Not "gym bro" — closer to the person who lifts at 5am, runs a
business, and reads. Aspirational-disciplined, not aspirational-wealthy.

## POSITIONING
Luxury training apparel. Gymshark's category, Aimé Leon Dore's finish.
Price above mass fitness brands, below designer. The gold foil and the fabric
weight are the proof — never claim premium, demonstrate it.

## VISUAL
Palette: Obsidian #0A0908 · Burgundy Reserve #5E1D25 · Sandstone Beige #B9A488 ·
Cream #F2EBDD · Gold #D8B678
Type: classical serif, wide letterspacing, all caps for the wordmark.
Mark: king piece + barbell in a circle. Gold foil on garments, never plastisol.
References: marble, gold leaf, low warm light. Never neon, never grunge, never gym-bro.

## VOICE
- Measured and quiet. Short sentences. Periods, not exclamation points.
- Confident without volume. The brand never shouts because it doesn't need to.
- Chess literacy assumed, used correctly, never explained.
- Talks about the trade-off roughly 60% of the time, the garment 40%.
- Specs persuade. 380gsm. YKK. Ninety washes. Adjectives don't.

**NEVER:** "insane," "fire," "obsessed," "beast mode," "no days off," "elevate,"
"curated," "essentials," "must-have." No hustle-culture grind-porn — we say
*calculated*, not *relentless*. No fake scarcity. Max one emoji, usually zero.
